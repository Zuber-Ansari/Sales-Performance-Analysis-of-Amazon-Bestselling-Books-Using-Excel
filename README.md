# Book-Store-Analysis-Using-Excel

This dashboard presents a consolidated view of bookstore performance from 2009 to 2019, focusing on pricing, customer engagement, ratings, and genre contribution.

+ Customer engagement is strong and growing, with total reviews reaching 6.57 million and clear upward trends over time, indicating increasing reader interaction and platform usage.

+ User satisfaction is consistently high, with an average rating of 4.62, improving steadily in later years despite falling prices.

+ Book pricing shows a long-term decline, suggesting a shift toward competitive pricing or market pressure, while maintaining quality as reflected in rising ratings.

+ Non-Fiction slightly outperforms Fiction in overall engagement, contributing 56.36% of total reviews, signaling higher demand for informational and practical content.

+ Top authors maintain near-perfect ratings, reflecting strong author credibility and loyal readership.

+ Interactive filters enable targeted analysis by genre, price, rating, and year, supporting flexible exploration and data-driven decision-making.
